Your chest is mainly made of two muscles — the pectoralis major and pectoralis minor. The major muscle has three parts: upper, middle, and lower. To build a full, wide chest, you must train all angles. Upper chest: incline bench or incline dumbbell press. Middle chest: flat bench press or push-ups. Lower chest: decline press or dips. For growth, lift heavy but controlled, aiming for 8–12 reps per set. Focus on the mind–muscle connection — feel the chest contract every rep. Train 2 times a week, giving it 2–3 days to recover between sessions. Eat enough protein (1.6–2.2g per kg body weight) and sleep 7–9 hours to let your muscles rebuild. Use progressive overload — slowly increase weight or reps. For the outer chest, use wide-grip presses; for the inner chest, use close-grip or cable crossovers. Stay consistent, train with good form, rest well, and your chest will grow fuller, stronger, and more balanced.

The biceps (front) flex your arm, while the triceps (back) make up most of your arm’s size. The forearms support grip and shape. For biceps, do barbell curls, dumbbell curls, and hammer curls. For triceps, hit skull crushers, close-grip bench press, and pushdowns. For forearms, do wrist curls and farmer’s holds. Train arms 2 times a week, with 2–3 exercises for each side. Focus on form, squeeze, and slow control. Rest 48 hours before hitting them again. Eat well, sleep enough, and your arms will grow full and defined

Your legs are made of the quadriceps (front thighs), hamstrings (back thighs), glutes (butt), and calves. To grow them, focus on compound lifts like squats, lunges, leg press, and deadlifts. For detail and shape, add leg extensions, leg curls, and calf raises. Train legs 1–2 times a week, 3–5 exercises, 8–12 reps per set. Rest 2–3 days between sessions — legs need recovery since they’re large muscles. Eat plenty of carbs and protein, and don’t skip leg day — it builds power, balance, and releases growth hormones that help your whole body grow.

Your abs include the rectus abdominis (six-pack), obliques (sides), and transverse abdominis (deep core). To build strong, visible abs, mix resistance work (weighted sit-ups, hanging leg raises, cable crunches) with bodyweight moves (planks, mountain climbers). Train abs 3–4 times a week, and keep your diet clean — abs are revealed by low body fat. Recovery is fast; they heal within 24 hours, so you can train them often. Don’t forget: abs are made in the gym and the kitchen 🍎

Your shoulders have three heads — front (anterior), side (lateral), and rear (posterior). To make them round and big, hit all angles: overhead press for front, lateral raises for side width, and rear delt flyes for back detail. Add upright rows for extra volume. Train shoulders 1–2 times a week with 3–4 exercises. Keep form strict to protect joints, and rest 48–72 hours before hitting them again. Eat enough protein, stay consistent, and you’ll get that wide, capped-shoulder look that makes your chest and arms pop. 🔥
